[MOD RELEASE] No More Heroes Paradise | Perfect Edition |

This is a mod built for RPCS3 & requires you to have a legal original copy of the game.

Requirements:
RPCS3
No More Heroes - Heroes Paradise (USA Version)

Install:
Just drag and drop the files in the required locations (See the readme in the release for details)

Details:
No More Heroes Paradise on PS3 is the best version of No More Heroes, but it’s always held back by some stupid decisions. 

After months of work, today I bring you No More Heroes Paradise - | Perfect Edition |

This mod features TONs of QOL adjustments to make this, in my opinion, the definitive way to experience No More Heroes.

Updates:
30FPS Frame rate unlocked to 60FPS resulting in much smoother gameplay.

Motion Controls COMPLETELY REMOVED:
Sword recharge is now the shacking the right stick (Like on the PC Version)
Mini Games with previous motion controls have been updated with relevant stick movements.

Original OST Restored:
Heavenly Star RESTORED to the shop music
Heavenly Star RESTORED to the games cutscenes (When Heavenly Star was removed, some cutscenes where just left with dialog only, this restores the music in those)

Remove Inputs:
R3 is no longer required to press before a finisher, and you are directly prompted to move the analog stick in the slash direction (Like on the PC version) 
R3 & L3 are no longer required to be pressed before a wrestling move, and you are directly prompted to move the analog stick (Like on the PC version)

QOL Updates:
Colors are adjusted so the contrast is less blown out. PS3 by default has an extremely high gamma multiplier on the textures. This makes colors closer to the original version + a closer match to the Xbox360 version, which looks the best.

Sword Clash now can use the left analog stick as well as the right analog stick for rotation.

Old motion gesture sprites are replaced with appropriate stick movements. 

Why do this? Because, apart from the above, The PS3 version also has tons of HUGE improvements over the PC / Wii / Switch version which are:
Store Dark Side Buffs 
Mission Teleport
Failed Instant Retires
Boss Battle Mode
No More Heroes 2 Extra Bosses
5 New Mini Games
5 New Hit Missions
Quick Map Onscreen Toggle
Map Can Be Used On Bike
Removed Pixelation Filter
Compacted & Improved Fidelity of Open World
Mission Replay Boss / Cut-scene replay
Movement When Using Charge Attacks
Wrestling Moves Titles Are Announced When Used
Get Collectibles While On Vehicle 
Double Roulettes On Multikills
